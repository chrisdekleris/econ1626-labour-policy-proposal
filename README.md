# AI and the Australian Labour Market: A Policy Proposal

ECON1626 Economics of Artificial Intelligence, RMIT University, Semester 2 2026
Assessment 2: Policy proposal (individual)

> **Status:** final version for submission. Due Sunday 27 September 2026, 23:59 AEST.

## What this is

A policy brief advising an Australian Government department on how to respond to AI's impacts on the labour market. It covers the channels through which AI changes work, the likely effects on employment, tasks, wages and industry structure, a set of policy options evaluated against explicit criteria, and a recommended, costed package with an implementation plan.

## How this repo was built

Each section of the brief has its own [Issue](https://github.com/chrisdekleris/econ1626-labour-policy-proposal/issues), and the commit that completes a section closes its Issue. The final evidence check goes through a pull request. Reading the commit history from the bottom up shows the brief being built section by section.

To read it: open [Commits](https://github.com/chrisdekleris/econ1626-labour-policy-proposal/commits/main) for the sequence, [closed Issues](https://github.com/chrisdekleris/econ1626-labour-policy-proposal/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aclosed) for what each step was meant to do, and [PR #10](https://github.com/chrisdekleris/econ1626-labour-policy-proposal/pull/10) for the evidence check that corrected two claims against their sources.
